A 10×10 cursor draws onto a white canvas held in a full-screen 40 KB framebuffer. Hold ✎ (PC5) to paint the current colour, ◑ (PC4) cycles the 6-colour palette, ⌫ (PA10) clears. The live swatch (top-right) shows the active colour.

Static design mockup at the real panel resolution; the six paint colours are the exact RGB565 palette constants from the firmware.
